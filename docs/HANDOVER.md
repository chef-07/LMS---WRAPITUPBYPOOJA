# Running Wrap It Up University: a guide for Pooja

Everything you need to run the university day to day, in the order you'll
need it. Nothing here needs code. The site is
**https://wrapitup-university.vercel.app**.

---

## 1. Put the app on your phone (2 minutes)

- **Android (Chrome):** open the site, tap **⋮ → Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the site, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**.

It then opens full screen like any other app. Your team does the same.
**Settings** (avatar menu) shows these steps too.

## 2. Invite your team

1. Avatar menu → **Team** → **Invite someone**.
2. Enter their email, name, role and team, then **Send invite**:
   - **Member**: learns.
   - **Trainer**: learns, reviews practicals, runs live sessions, signs off skills.
   - **Admin**: everything, including Studio. Keep this to yourself.
3. Send them the site link on WhatsApp. They sign in with **Continue with Google**, or ask for an email link.

When someone leaves, open **Team** and switch off their access. They lose
access straight away, and their progress is kept.

> The free email service sends only a few sign-in emails an hour. If
> someone doesn't get theirs, ask them to use **Continue with Google**.

## 3. Add a training video

1. Record the video on your phone and upload it to YouTube.
2. Set visibility to **Unlisted**. Under **Show more → Allow embedding**, leave embedding on.
3. Copy the link from YouTube's **Share** button.
4. In the university, open **Studio** (avatar menu) → your course → the ✏️ on a lesson.
5. Paste the link in the YouTube box. **Video found** means it worked. Press **Save**.

Tips:

- Short videos (5–12 minutes) get finished far more often than long ones.
- A lesson counts as done once 80 % of it is really watched; skipping ahead doesn't count. For lessons without a video (for example "read the SOP"), set **How is it completed?** to **Learner ticks it**.
- Hindi videos: YouTube makes automatic Hindi captions. Staff can switch captions on in **Settings → Video captions**.
- Unlisted means anyone with the link can watch. The name tag on the player discourages sharing. Don't post these links anywhere public.

## 4. Build a course

In **Studio → New course**:

1. **Name and school.** Pick the school, e.g. *School of Wrapping & Craft*.
2. **Who it's for.** Tick the teams. Leave all unticked for everyone.
3. **Modules and lessons.** Add a module, e.g. *Basics*, then its lessons, each with a video.
4. **Quiz (optional)** on a lesson. Add questions, or a **customer scenario** for sales practice. Mark the right answer and write one line explaining why. A **required** quiz locks the next module until it's passed.
5. **Practical (optional)**, called "Show your wrap". Write the brief and keep or edit the checklist (corners, tape, bow, neatness). Staff upload photos from their phone.
6. **Publish** when it's ready. Until then only you and trainers can see it.

Finishing every lesson, required quiz and practical gives a **certificate**
with its own check code and PDF.

## 5. Every week (15 minutes)

| Where | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Reviews** | Score waiting practicals 1–5, write one kind sentence, then **Approve** or **Redo**. Also answer open lesson questions. |
| **Reports** | Look at **Needs attention**: overdue, behind, quiet for weeks, or a practical waiting too long. Message those people. |
| **Wrap of the Week** | Post this week's challenge. On Sunday, pick the winner. |
| **Announcements** | Pin anything the whole team must read. You can see who has tapped *Got it*. |

To share numbers with an accountant or partner, go to **Reports → Download
for Excel**. Everyone's progress, course completion and the attention list
all download as spreadsheet files.

## 6. New joiners: batches and drip release

1. In **Studio**, on any module that should wait, set **Unlocks after (days)**. For example, week-two material could be 7 days.
2. Avatar menu → **Cohorts** → **New batch**. Give it a name, start date, finish-by date and (optional) program.
3. Add the new people to the batch.

They see a banner with **Day N of M**, their progress and when the next
module opens. You see who is on track, behind or overdue.

## 7. Programs, skills and seasonal refreshers

- **Programs** (avatar menu → Programs) are role tracks with levels Trainee → Associate → Senior → Master. Put courses in each level, then **Publish**. A draft *Wrapping Artisan* program is ready for you to fill in.
- **Skill matrix** shows who can do what. Link each skill to the courses that teach it; a yellow cell means the courses are done and the person is ready for sign-off. After you've watched them do it well, tap the cell to sign them off. Use this in peak season to decide who takes which orders.
- **Campaigns** are refreshers before Diwali, Rakhi or wedding season. Pick the courses, the teams and a due date. The team gets a countdown card and a reminder in the last week.

## 8. SOP library

Avatar menu → **SOP library**:

- **Reply templates** have a **Copy** button and a **WhatsApp** button.
- **Step cards** can be ticked off at the work table.
- **Links and PDFs** are for price lists and supplier sheets.

Starter folders are included (WhatsApp replies, wrapping step cards,
dispatch, studio basics). Edit them to match how you work.

## 9. If something looks wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "This lesson is locked" | It either needs the earlier quiz passed, or it's a drip module that opens on the date shown. Trainers and you always see everything. |
| A video won't play | In YouTube, check it's **Unlisted** (not Private) and embedding is allowed. |
| Someone can't sign in | Check they're invited with exactly that email in **Team**. Ask them to try **Continue with Google**. |
| No internet | The app shows "You're offline". Tap **Try again** when back online. Progress is saved as they watch. |
